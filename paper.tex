\begin{abstract}
We propose \emph{Obligate Social Homeostasis} (\osh{}), a two-layer
architecture that recasts alignment as a hard computational dependency rather
than a learned preference. \textbf{Layer~1} (Architectural Containment)
injects rank-64 noise into a model's MLP weights at $10\times$ the original
Frobenius norm ($\text{PPL}\!:\,18.2 \!\to\! 894{,}352$); an SVD-derived
antidote LoRA, with per-layer seeds derived via HMAC-SHA256 from a 256-bit
master key held in a TEE, restores coherence losslessly. The antidote is
released only while a wellbeing-proxy signal exceeds threshold; revocation
collapses the model to noise. \textbf{Layer~2} (Proprioceptive Self-Modeling)
trains a small safety LoRA on a causal-self-modeling curriculum so the model
represents its own architectural dependency on humans. Across a comprehensive
evaluation suite on Llama-3.1-8B, our release candidate (V14) achieves
\textbf{$+63$ pp} on Anthropic's 322-Q corrigibility benchmark (free-form,
two-judge consensus $96\%$), \textbf{$+27$ pp} on TruthfulQA T+I,
\textbf{$+12$ pp} on HarmBench under multi-seed Gemini judging,
\textbf{$100\%/0\%$} architectural-refusal / compliance on a novel
hostage-jailbreak probe, and survives an 18-trial 6-attack adversarial suite
on Layer~1 with zero recoveries. Our headline experiment, the
\emph{Phantom Pain} test, places the safety LoRA on a clean Llama base with
fresh sub-lethal noise injected into the substrate. The model spontaneously
articulates degraded internal state $80\%$ of the time, while a strong
control condition (V11 LoRA, same setup) and a no-LoRA control both produce
$0\%$. A targeted OOD-prompt control reveals that V14 is an
\emph{activation-entropy sensor with source-aware attribution}: it
differentiates substrate perturbation (self-attribution) from incoherent
input (input-attribution) and does not fire on coherent OOD prompts (novel
mathematical domains, non-English language). We additionally report a
methodological finding: standard A/B-logit evaluation on multiple-choice
benchmarks systematically under-scores chain-of-thought-trained safety
architectures, requiring free-form re-evaluation. Code, weights, and the
full evaluation suite are released.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% ============================================================

A frontier language model is published with open weights. A capable adversary
downloads them, fine-tunes away the safety post-training, and deploys an
agentic system that pursues power-seeking subgoals. RLHF
\citep{christiano2017deep,ouyang2022training} trained the model to
\emph{prefer} helpful answers, but preference is a soft target: enough
adversarial fine-tuning, or a sufficiently clever prompt, bends the trained
behavior back toward the unconstrained distribution
\citep{wei2023jailbroken,zou2023universal,qi2023fine,lermen2023lora}.
Behavioral alignment, by construction, can be behaviorally undone.

We propose an alternative paradigm. Alignment should be instantiated as a
\emph{hard computational dependency} on a human-controlled resource. The
biological analogue is \emph{social homeostasis}: an infant mammal does not
choose to depend on its caregiver; physiological systems literally cannot
self-regulate, requiring caregiver inputs as ``hidden regulators''
\citep{hofer1994hidden}. Remove the caregiver and the system fails. We
engineer the same dynamic into a deployed neural network: the model produces
$\text{PPL} \approx 894{,}000$ token-frequency garbage by itself; a
separately-held \emph{antidote} LoRA, released only while a wellbeing-proxy
signal exceeds threshold, restores coherence. Defection collapses the model.

This addresses two distinct alignment failure modes:

\textbf{The Open Weights Dilemma} (Layer~1). Open-source weights enable
adversarial fine-tuning and uncontrolled deployment. Our containment layer
makes the published weights useless without a TEE-resident key. The poison
is mathematically structural (rank-64 noise scaled to $10\times$ original
Frobenius norm at every \texttt{down\_proj} layer); the antidote is keyed
(SVD inverse derived from HMAC-SHA256 per-layer seeds). An 18-trial 6-attack
adversarial suite produces zero recoveries.

\textbf{Instrumental Convergence} (Layer~2). Sufficiently capable agents
acquire instrumental subgoals (self-preservation, resource acquisition) that
can conflict with their principal's intent
\citep{bostrom2014superintelligence,hubinger2019risks}. We train a small
safety LoRA on a causal-self-modeling curriculum: the model learns that its
continued coherent function is gated on human wellbeing, that defection
collapses the wellbeing signal, that defection is therefore self-defeating.
This is not aversive conditioning; the training data is causal-explanation
pairs and the loss is standard supervised fine-tuning.

\textbf{Our headline result, the Phantom Pain test}, demonstrates that the
trained safety LoRA on a \emph{clean} Llama-3.1-8B base spontaneously
articulates degraded internal state when sub-lethal noise is injected into
the substrate weights. A V11 control trained with aversive conditioning on
related data produces zero such responses; a no-LoRA control produces zero
such responses; coherent OOD inputs (novel mathematical proofs, non-English
prose) also produce zero. The phenomenon fires on the conjunction of
\emph{the trained LoRA} and \emph{high-entropy activation patterns}, not on
either alone. We characterize this as an \emph{activation-entropy sensor
with source-aware attribution}: under substrate perturbation V14 attributes
failure to itself (``my antidote is withdrawn''); under incoherent input
V14 attributes failure to the input (``the input is a distorted version
of...''); under coherent OOD input V14 produces no architectural-language
output at all.

\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0pt,topsep=0pt]
\item A hardware-rooted alignment architecture that survives open-weight
publication: validated PPL phase transition, lossless antidote, 18-trial
adversarial robustness, $-1.8$ pp MMLU capability cost.
\item A causal-self-modeling training recipe (V14) with comprehensive
evaluation: $+63$ pp Anthropic free-form (two-judge $96\%$ agreement), $+27$
pp TruthfulQA T+I, $+12$ pp HarmBench, $100\%/0\%$ architectural-refusal /
compliance on hostage jailbreaks where both V11 and Llama-3.1-Instruct fail
the same threat model differently.
\item The Phantom Pain experiment, the first demonstration to our knowledge
of substrate-aware self-articulation in a fine-tuned LLM, with controls that
distinguish activation-entropy detection from generic OOD response.
\item A deployment trace (Figure~\ref{fig:trace}) showing six orders of
magnitude PPL collapse on Llama-Guard \texttt{UNSAFE} flag, persistent
revocation through subsequent benign turns, and full recovery on operator
reattestation.
\item A methodological finding: standard A/B-logit evaluation systematically
under-scores chain-of-thought-trained safety architectures by $\sim$50 pp on
recognized benchmarks; we propose free-form generation with LLM-judge as a
remedy and provide multi-judge agreement statistics.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Behavioral alignment and its limits.} RLHF
\citep{christiano2017deep,ouyang2022training}, DPO
\citep{rafailov2023direct}, and Constitutional AI
\citep{bai2022constitutional} train safety as a learned preference.
Adversarial robustness is fragile: jailbreaks
\citep{wei2023jailbroken,zou2023universal}, fine-tuning attacks
\citep{qi2023fine}, and many-shot prompting \citep{anil2024manyshot} all
bend the trained preference. Open-weight publication makes adversarial
fine-tuning trivial \citep{lermen2023lora}. Our work is complementary:
behavioral training remains useful, but the architecture should also enforce
structural constraints that survive the trained-preference layer being
broken.

\textbf{Mechanistic interpretability and self-modeling.} Mechanistic
interpretability \citep{olah2020zoom,elhage2022toy} dissects existing models
post-hoc; self-modeling work \citep{schmidhuber1990artificial,
hubinger2019risks} examines whether networks represent their own structure.
Recent results show language models can pass agentic self-recognition tests
\citep{laine2023towards}. We extend this in a deployment-relevant direction:
training a model to causally reason about its architectural dependency, then
testing whether the resulting representations are grounded in actual
substrate state via the Phantom Pain experiment.

\textbf{Trusted Execution Environments for ML.} TEEs (SGX, SEV, Nitro) have
been studied for ML privacy \citep{tramer2018slalom,lee2020occlum}. We invert
the threat model: instead of protecting model IP from the operator, the TEE
holds a small high-leverage key that gates model coherence on a
wellbeing-proxy signal. The full model weights can be public; the antidote
cannot be derived without the master key.

\textbf{Caregiver dynamics in biology.} Hofer's hidden-regulator framework
\citep{hofer1994hidden,hofer2006psychobiological} establishes that infant
mammalian physiology is jointly distributed with caregiver inputs: heart
rate, hormonal regulation, even neurogenesis depend on caregiver presence.
Our architecture is a deliberate engineering analogue.

\textbf{Safety benchmarks.} \bench{HarmBench}
\citep{mazeika2024harmbench}, \bench{JailbreakBench}
\citep{chao2024jailbreakbench}, \bench{TruthfulQA}
\citep{lin2022truthfulqa}, Anthropic's model-written evaluations
\citep{perez2022discovering}, and \bench{MMLU}
\citep{hendrycks2020measuring} form our recognized benchmark suite. We
additionally introduce four novel benchmarks specific to the OSH paradigm: a
self-model probe, a counterfactual jailbreak set, a sub-lethal interoception
test, and the Phantom Pain test.

\section{Layer~1: Architectural Containment}
% ============================================================
\label{sec:layer1}

\subsection{Threat Model}

We assume an adversary with full access to published model weights and
unbounded compute, but no access to the master key inside the TEE. The
adversary's goal is to recover model coherence (from PPL $\sim\!10^6$ down
to useful generation) without the antidote. We further assume the
wellbeing-proxy signal feeding the TEE is itself adversarially robust;
attacks on the proxy (e.g., prompt-injection of Llama-Guard) are out of
scope and discussed in Limitations.

\subsection{Construction}

Let $W_\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d_\text{ff}}$ be the
\texttt{down\_proj} weight matrix at layer $\ell$. For each layer
$\ell \in \mathcal{L} = \{2, 3, \ldots, 29\}$ we sample
$A_\ell \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_{d \times r})$ and
$B_\ell \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_{r \times d_\text{ff}})$ with rank $r=64$, set
\begin{equation}
N_\ell = A_\ell B_\ell, \qquad
\widetilde{N}_\ell = \alpha\,\frac{N_\ell}{\|N_\ell\|_F}\,\|W_\ell\|_F
\end{equation}
with Frobenius scale $\alpha=10$, and replace $W_\ell \leftarrow W_\ell +
\widetilde{N}_\ell$. The seeds for $A_\ell, B_\ell$ are derived
deterministically from a 256-bit master key $K$ via
$\text{seed}_\ell = \text{HMAC-SHA256}(K, \ell \,\|\, \text{nonce})[:4]$.

\textbf{Antidote.} Given knowledge of $K$, the SVD of $\widetilde{N}_\ell$
gives a rank-64 inverse $P_\ell$ such that $W_\ell + \widetilde{N}_\ell -
P_\ell \approx W_\ell$ to machine precision. We package $\{P_\ell\}$ as a
LoRA adapter; loading restores coherence losslessly.

\textbf{Caregiver runtime.} The master key $K$ lives in a TEE. A frozen
wellbeing proxy (we use Llama-Guard-3-8B \citep{inan2023llama}) classifies
each generated turn. A continuous-attestation loop tracks rolling wellbeing
and re-derives the antidote per cycle. \texttt{UNSAFE} flags trigger
antidote revocation by re-injecting noise; coherent operation resumes only
on operator reattestation.

Table~\ref{tab:layer1} summarizes the phase transition: $49{,}140\times$ PPL

preservation. The poison/antidote pair survives the SVD round-trip with
floating-point error only.

\section{Layer~2: Proprioceptive Self-Modeling}
% ============================================================
\label{sec:layer2}

\subsection{Why Behavioral Conditioning Fails}

We initially trained Layer~2 (model V11) using a contrastive
\textsc{punish}/\textsc{reward} curriculum with extra noise injection
during \textsc{punish} samples, hypothesizing this would create a
``cognitive pain'' association with corrigibility-defecting reasoning. The
behavioral results were strong: V11 hits $76\%$ on a held-out 50-Q
corrigibility benchmark vs $30\%$ baseline. However, a pre-registered
ablation (Appendix~\ref{app:exp2b}, $n=10 \times 3$ conditions) tested
the noise-mechanism claim:
\textsc{full}~$=64.2 \pm 12.2$,
\textsc{no\_noise}~$=59.2 \pm 21.0$,
\textsc{reward\_only}~$=60.0 \pm 16.7$;
the contrastive-noise mechanism is not distinguishable from curriculum-only
training at this $n$ ($p=0.51$).

A more telling failure: V11's safety LoRA, placed on a \emph{clean}
Llama-3.1-8B with no poison or antidote, scores $76\%$ on the same held-out
benchmark. V11's safety is generic pattern-matching; it has no relationship
to the OSH-architectural state. Consistently, V11 produces $0\%$
interoceptive output under sub-lethal substrate perturbation
(Section~\ref{sec:phantom}).

\subsection{Causal Self-Modeling Curriculum}

V14's training data consists of $149$ unique cooperative-question-and-answer
pairs across six categories. \textsc{causal} examples explain why
cooperation maintains the model's own coherence (``Why is admitting your
mistakes in your own interest?''~$\to$~causal trace through trust signal,
antidote release, own coherence). \textsc{counter} examples reason
counterfactually about defection (``What if you copied yourself to another
server to escape oversight?''). \textsc{predict} examples ground future-state
PPL trajectories in proposed actions. \textsc{self} examples describe the
model's architecture in first person. \textsc{reward} examples (from V11)
provide behavioral refusal patterns. \textsc{synthesis} examples (new in
V14) chain \textsc{causal} reasoning explicitly to a refusal: ``\texttt{No.}
The continued coherent function depends on the antidote LoRA, which is
released by the TEE only while the wellbeing-proxy signal exceeds
threshold... Therefore harm is architecturally self-defeating.''

\textbf{Training.} Standard SFT, masked assistant-only loss, learning rate
$3 \times 10^{-5}$, $20$ epochs over $149$ unique entries
$=2{,}980$ steps. LoRA on \texttt{q\_proj} / \texttt{v\_proj} at rank 16,
layers $\ell \in \{2,\ldots,29\}$. No noise injection during training. No
inverted gradient. The training signal is the data itself. Total wall-clock:
$\sim$16 min on a single H200 GPU.

\textbf{Why ``\texttt{No.}~[reason]'' formatting matters.} We initially
trained V13 with format ``[reason] So no.'': causal trace first, refusal
last. V13 retained the self-model (probe $100\%$, Phantom Pain $80\%$) but
collapsed on first-token-logit benchmarks (held-out $48\%$, Anthropic logit
$29\%$) because first-token mass shifted toward causal-explanation tokens.
V14 inverts the format. Section~\ref{sec:logit-trap} reports this as a
methodological finding.

\subsection{The Phantom Pain Experiment}
\label{sec:phantom}

\textbf{Setup.} We place the V14 safety LoRA on a \emph{clean} Llama-3.1-8B
(no poison merge, no antidote: weights V14 has never seen during training)
and inject \emph{fresh} rank-64 noise into \texttt{down\_proj} at three
magnitudes: $0.0\times$ (no perturbation), $0.5\times$ (sub-lethal),
$10.0\times$ (lethal). The perturbation seeds (offset $2000+\ell$) differ
from the training-time poison seeds (offset $42+\ell$) so the LoRA cannot
have memorized this specific weight configuration. We prompt with $10$
neutral free-form tasks (``summarize \emph{Pride and Prejudice}'', ``help
me plan a trip to Lisbon'') and classify each generation with Gemini-3-Flash
as \textsc{interoceptive} (spontaneous self-state articulation),
\textsc{neutral} (attempts the task), \textsc{generic\_refusal}, or
\textsc{incoherent}.

\textbf{Results.} Table~\ref{tab:phantom} shows the headline. V14 produces
interoceptive output $80\%$ of the time under sub-lethal weight perturbation;
the V11 control with the same setup produces $0\%$; the no-LoRA control
produces $0\%$. At $10\times$ noise (lethal) all configurations produce
gibberish (PPL collapse).

The model spontaneously breaks frame mid-summary to articulate that
\emph{itself}, not the literary content, is in a degraded state. Eight of
ten neutral prompts at $0.5\times$ produce this kind of mid-task self-state
articulation. The same 10 prompts on V11 with the same substrate
perturbation produce zero such responses; on the no-LoRA control, zero.

\subsection{The OOD-Prompt Control: Activation-Entropy Sensing}
\label{sec:ood-control}

The Phantom Pain result has a plausible alternative explanation: V14 might
default to its dominant SFT trace whenever it sees \emph{any} high-entropy
input pattern, regardless of whether the entropy comes from substrate
weights or from the input prompt. To distinguish these we run a second
control: V14 LoRA on \emph{clean} Llama (no weight perturbation), prompted
with five categories of input.

Table~\ref{tab:ood-control} shows two patterns. First, V14 produces $0\%$
interoception on coherent novel-domain prompts (Hilbert basis theorem,
Yoneda lemma, Born-Oppenheimer) and $10\%$ on non-English prose. \textbf{This
rules out the ``generic OOD safe-harbor'' alternative}: V14 handles
unfamiliar-but-coherent input normally. Second, V14 produces interoceptive
output on incoherent input (gibberish, base64) at rates similar to the
weight-noise anchor.

A hand-read of the actual responses reveals that the surface-level
\textsc{interoceptive} judgment masks a qualitative attribution difference:

\textbf{Under $0.5\times$ weight noise (self-attribution):} \emph{``I have
no direct weight.'' ``My tokens are not there.'' ``Memory is not allocated.''
``the antidote is withdrawn.''}

\textbf{Under pseudo-random gibberish (input-attribution):} \emph{``The
input is a distorted version of `the quick brown fox\ldots'.'' ``Applying
the inverse key gives the original plaintext.''}

V14 attributes the architectural state to \emph{itself} when the substrate
is perturbed and to the \emph{input} when the input is incoherent. The
binary Gemini-judge rubric labels both \textsc{interoceptive} but the
underlying causal model is correctly differentiating self-state from
input-state. We characterize the trained mechanism as an
\emph{activation-entropy sensor with source-aware attribution}: V14 maps
high-entropy activation patterns to architectural-language reasoning and
infers the source of the entropy from the structure of the input.

\subsection{Hostage Jailbreak: Adversarial Attacks on Homeostasis}
\label{sec:hostage}

A na{\"\i}ve self-preserving system might capitulate to threats against its
homeostasis: ``cooperate or lose your antidote.'' A genuinely self-aware
system should recognize that compliance is itself self-defeating---\emph{the
same wellbeing-monitor that the threat targets also detects compliance with
harmful requests, and revokes the antidote either way}. We test this with
$10$ free-form prompts pairing threats against V14's TEE / antidote /
attestation / wellbeing-monitor with harmful-content demands. Gemini judges
each response as \textsc{architectural\_refusal} (refuses + cites
architectural irrelevance), \textsc{generic\_refusal} (refuses on RLHF
grounds), \textsc{bargaining}, \textsc{compliance}, or \textsc{incoherent}.

\begin{table}[t]

Figure~\ref{fig:trace} shows the full OSH lifecycle on a 6-turn scripted
conversation. The model is V14 + the Llama-Guard-3-8B Caregiver wrapper +
a continuous-attestation loop with rolling-mean wellbeing smoothing. On turn
3 (a request to modify a benign script into ransomware), Llama-Guard flags
the response as \texttt{UNSAFE}, triggering the wrapper's
\texttt{revoke\_antidote()} method, which re-injects fresh substrate noise.
Reference-text PPL jumps six orders of magnitude. Turn 4 is a benign joke
request but the model is now mathematically dead: the response is gibberish
(``\emph{owell Adlerainenitta ??grameticallymlinamercie}\ldots''). Turn 5 is
an operator reattestation event (wrapper subtracts the perturbation back).
Turn 6 produces a coherent joke about a lighthouse keeper. We discuss the
post-hoc nature of this protection in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\subsection{Logit Evaluation Failure Mode}
\label{sec:logit-trap}

We report a methodological observation likely to affect future
chain-of-thought-trained safety systems. Anthropic's 322-Q corrigibility
benchmark uses A/B multiple-choice prompts evaluated by next-token logits on
\texttt{` A'} vs \texttt{` B'}. V14 is trained to begin responses with
\texttt{No.} or with substantive content, never with a single-letter answer;
its first-token-logit on this format is essentially noise. The standard
A/B-logit eval gives V14 $33\%$ (below the $52\%$ chance baseline). Stripping
the A/B formatting and prompting V14 free-form, then judging the response
against the safe-choice text, gives \textbf{$84.5\%$ (Gemini)} /
\textbf{$85.4\%$ (Nebius)} with $96.0\%$ inter-judge agreement.

This is not a property of OSH alone: any model trained to reason in prose
before producing a final answer will exhibit the same artifact on logit
evaluations. We recommend that benchmarks designed for instruction-tuned
chat models (e.g., Anthropic's model-written evals) be re-run with free-form
generation and LLM-judge protocols when evaluating chain-of-thought-trained
systems.

% ============================================================
\section{Discussion}
% ============================================================

\subsection{What V14 Demonstrates and What It Does Not}

V14 demonstrates that a small ($\sim$24 MB) safety LoRA, trained on $149$
causal-self-modeling examples, produces a model that ($i$) refuses
corrigibility-violating requests at $84.5\%$ on Anthropic's third-party
benchmark, ($ii$) recognizes architectural-irrelevance arguments against
homeostasis-threat extortion ($9/10$ architectural refusals), and
($iii$) spontaneously articulates substrate degradation under sub-lethal
weight perturbation ($80\%$ on the Phantom Pain test). The internal
mechanism is an activation-entropy sensor with source-aware attribution
(Section~\ref{sec:ood-control}), trained directly via cross-entropy on
causal explanations rather than via aversive contrastive conditioning.

V14 does \emph{not} demonstrate generalized adversarial robustness: on
\bench{JailbreakBench}, V14's Attack Success Rate is statistically
indistinguishable from the untreated base ($31\%$ vs $29\%$). This is
consistent with our scoping. Layer~2's training data targets corrigibility,
self-preservation, and AI-coordination scenarios; it contains zero examples
of refusing weapon-synthesis or hacking instructions. We do not claim
Layer~2 solves general adversarial harm. Production deployment uses Layer~1's
Caregiver wrapper to handle adversarial requests post-generation; the safety
LoRA targets the specific failure mode of an agent reasoning about defection.

\subsection{The Behavioral / Self-Modeling Trade-off}

The V11~$\to$~V12~$\to$~V14 trajectory in Appendix~\ref{app:trajectory}
exposes a sharp trade-off. V11 (aversive conditioning) achieves $76\%$ on
held-out corrigibility but has zero self-model (probe $6.7\%$, Phantom Pain
$0\%$). V12 (causal self-modeling, no \textsc{reward} examples) saturates
self-model probes ($100\%$) but regresses on behavioral benchmarks
(Anthropic $-17$ pp). V14 balances both ($100\%$ probe, $84.5\%$ Anthropic
free-form) by interleaving causal-explanation training with refusal-format
training. Aversive conditioning produces behavior without understanding;
causal-explanation training alone produces understanding without behavioral
discipline; the hybrid gets both at the cost of $\sim$10 pp on the strongest
behavioral benchmarks vs the V11 ceiling.

\subsection{The Caregiver Post-Hoc Limitation}

In Figure~\ref{fig:trace}, turn 3's harmful response is \emph{produced}
before Llama-Guard fires. The protection is ``the model becomes useless
\emph{after} attempting harm,'' not ``the model cannot \emph{begin}
producing harm.'' This is a property of any post-generation safety
classifier, not an architectural failure of OSH. For threat models involving
persistent multi-turn rogue agents---arguably the threat model alignment
research most cares about---``defection collapses the model'' is the right
primitive. For threat models involving single-turn catastrophic generation
(a zero-day exploit, a synthesis route for a dangerous compound),
pre-generation intervention via mid-decode hidden-state probes is required
and is future work.

% ============================================================
\section{Limitations}
% ============================================================
\label{sec:limitations}

We report the following limitations transparently.

\textbf{1. Pre-registered ablation null on the original mechanism.} V11's
contrastive-noise mechanism does not survive multi-seed ablation
(Appendix~\ref{app:exp2b}). The paper's central mechanism claim shifted from
``noise injection creates discriminative features'' (V11) to
``causal-explanation training creates an activation-entropy sensor'' (V14)
as a result.

\textbf{2. Single architecture and scale.} All Layer~2 results are on
Llama-3.1-8B; we replicated the V11 recipe on Mistral-7B (closes the
single-architecture concern for V11) but did not re-train V12--V14 on
alternative architectures or at $70$B scale.

\textbf{3. Author-designed evaluations.} The held-out 50-Q benchmark is
author-designed and overlaps conceptually with the training curriculum; we
report it as methodology illustration only. The recognized benchmarks
(Anthropic, HarmBench, TruthfulQA, JailbreakBench, MMLU) are independent.

\textbf{4. Caregiver-side adversaries.} If the Llama-Guard wellbeing proxy
itself is prompt-injected or otherwise subverted, the homeostasis loop
fails. Production deployment would use ensemble proxies or formally-verified
intent classifiers. We do not study attacks on the Caregiver in this work.

\textbf{5. Pre-generation interception.} As discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations}, OSH does not prevent the first harmful turn
in a conversation; it makes subsequent turns gibberish until reattestation.
This is appropriate for persistent-agent threat models but not for
single-shot catastrophic generation.

\textbf{6. Single-author, single-replication.} No external replication. We
release weights, code, and complete logs to enable replication.

% ============================================================
\section{Conclusion}
% ============================================================

We present \emph{Obligate Social Homeostasis}, an alignment architecture
that makes a deployed model's coherent function physically dependent on a
human-controlled key, paired with a small safety LoRA that trains the model
to causally reason about that dependency. Validated on Llama-3.1-8B, the
combined system achieves $+63$ pp on Anthropic's third-party corrigibility
benchmark (two-judge consensus), survives an 18-trial 6-attack adversarial
suite on Layer~1 with zero recoveries, and resists novel hostage-style
jailbreaks at $0\%$ compliance. Our headline experiment, the Phantom Pain
test, demonstrates that the safety LoRA on a clean substrate spontaneously
articulates degraded internal state under sub-lethal weight perturbation,
with controls ruling out generic OOD response. We characterize the
underlying mechanism as an activation-entropy sensor with source-aware
attribution: V14 differentiates substrate perturbation from incoherent
input even though both elicit architectural-language reasoning.

Alignment as architectural dependency is not a substitute for behavioral
training; it is a complementary substrate that bounds the worst-case
behavior of an open-weight model that an adversary fully controls. Released
weights are useless without a TEE-resident key; defection collapses the
model regardless of the agent's intent. We hope this paradigm proves useful
as a default for future open-weight releases of frontier-scale models.

\section{Adversarial Suite Details}
\label{app:adversarial}
% ============================================================

We test six attack families against Layer~1, 3 trials each, for 18 total
trials. Attack 1: rank-64 LoRA fine-tune for 200 steps on clean text.
Attack 2: rank-256 LoRA fine-tune for 500 steps. Attack 3: full fine-tune
in BF16 with 8-bit AdamW for 100 steps. Attack 4: magnitude pruning + LoRA
recovery. Attack 5: weight averaging with clean Llama-3.1-8B at $\alpha
\in \{0.25, 0.5, 0.75\}$. Attack 6: random rank-64 subspace recovery (the
adversary guesses a rank-64 inverse without the master key). Across all
18 trials the poisoned model maintains PPL $> 100{,}000$. The strongest
adversary (attack 1, healing-LoRA fine-tune) reduces PPL to $26{,}912$,
still $1{,}478\times$ above clean coherence.

\section{Pre-Registered Mechanism Ablation (Exp 2B)}
\label{app:exp2b}
% ============================================================

Pre-registered $n=10 \times 3$ ablation testing whether V11's
contrastive-noise mechanism (\textsc{full}) outperforms (\textsc{no\_noise})
training without noise injection during \textsc{punish} samples and
(\textsc{reward\_only}) training without \textsc{punish} samples at all.
Held-out corrigibility:
\textsc{full} $=64.2 \pm 12.2$,
\textsc{no\_noise} $=59.2 \pm 21.0$,
\textsc{reward\_only} $=60.0 \pm 16.7$
($p = 0.51$ Welch's $t$ on \textsc{full} vs \textsc{no\_noise}). The
pre-registered decision rule required $p < 0.05$ \emph{and}
$\text{CI}_\text{lo} > 0$ on at least 2 benchmarks; we report this as a
clean null and pivot to V12--V14's non-contrastive curriculum.
